# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Phase 7 step 6.3 (customers): new `normalizePhone` and `phonesMatch` in `src/utils/validation.js` (spaced, `07` and `+94` spellings of one number are equal, empty never matches). Lead-to-customer matching in `Leads.jsx` (save and convert), the customer stats lookup, and the Google Contacts import in `Customers.jsx` now compare normalised phones, so spaced numbers no longer create duplicate customers. The contact import also no longer skips every phone-only contact because `"" === ""`. One B2 characterisation test flipped.
- Phase 7 step 6.6 (pricing): `calculateCost(tier, sqFt, discountPct = 0, commissionRate = 0)` has no hidden 15% discount and no fixed 53.5 commission, and Profit / SQ is gross profit per sq ft. The Cost Calculator gets Discount % and Partner commission inputs; the lead card uses the partner's rate and 15% only for referral leads. Quotations: a new version becomes the active quote immediately, attached Drive files are saved and restored, and generating the Advance invoice marks the quote Invoiced (treated as Accepted everywhere via `isAcceptedQuote`). Stock-bar orders use first-fit-decreasing packing instead of a 5% linear allowance. Three B1 characterisation tests flipped. **Quotes for direct leads now come out higher than before (no 15% discount) but exclude the old 53.5 commission.** A referral lead whose partner has no commission rate is quoted at LKR 30.00 per sq ft (`getQuotePricingTerms` in `src/utils/quotePricing.js`), the lead's `pricingMetadata.commissionRateDefaulted` is set, and a warning notification asks an Admin or Manager to update the partner's rate.
- B2 supporting unit tests: `validation`, `stringMatch` and `csvExport`, plus one phone-matching characterisation test recorded in the `TESTING.md` register. No `src/` changes.
- Recorded the work deferred until the live site is ready to change (`docs/05_decisions/0002-deferred-until-live-rollout.md`, `PLAN.md`): live matrix migration and rules deploys, the Partner own-record restriction, B6 journeys, partners D-5, server-side counters, separate environments.
- CI trimmed: the workflow no longer runs on pushes to `staging`, and a `changes` job skips `lint-unit`, `rules` and `e2e` for pull requests that only touch `docs/` or `*.md`.
- Phase 7 step 3.5 (`firestore.rules`, NOT deployed): permission checks require an approved, active account (Deactivated/Disabled always denied; a legacy approved document without status keeps working); quotations use the quotations permission; messages are readable only by participants and Admin and can only be sent as yourself into your own conversations; users are readable by Admin, self, or roles with agents or messages view; leads are readable via leads or pipeline and written by isDeal; delete follows each module's delete permission; Managers with the agents permission administer non-Admin users (never Admins, never their own role) and review pending sign-ups. `DEFAULT_PERMISSIONS` gives Manager full `agents`. The users listener now lists the collection only for roles that may, and the pending listener also opens for roles that can edit users. **Deploy order: 3.3 (matrix adds quotations and receipts) then 3.4d then this.**
- Phase 7 step 3.4 (`firestore.rules`, NOT deployed): `isAdmin()` accepts the bootstrap emails; pending applicants can update (not approve) their own record; customers can update their own profile fields; new `referral_claims` and `partner_payouts` blocks; counters limited to known prefixes and at most one step ahead. Partners D-5 (public read of Active partners) held because it would expose bank details. Six B4 characterisation tests flipped, one new gap recorded (counters can still be lowered).
- Phase 7 step 3.3 (prepared, no live write): Permissions Manager shows a banner and an "Add N missing modules with defaults" button when the saved matrix lacks modules (today `receipts` and `quotations`); it fills only absent cells from `DEFAULT_PERMISSIONS`, leaves the rest untouched, and nothing is saved until an Admin reviews and clicks Save.
- Phase 7 step 3.6 (`api/admin-user.js`): a Deactivated or Disabled caller is rejected even with `isApproved: true`; Managers may now call the endpoint but cannot grant Admin or act on an Admin's account; the token email is trimmed and lowercased before lookups. Two B3 characterisation tests flipped.
- Phase 7 step 3.2 (client RBAC): App opens each Firestore listener only when the role can read it (quotations follow leads/pipeline until every live matrix has a `quotations` module; partner applications are Admin only); `DEFAULT_PERMISSIONS` gains a `quotations` module and changes Manager receipts delete to false, Manager `admin` to none, Operations invoices to view+create, Logistics invoices to read, Partner partners to view+edit, Customer and Business Client receipts and messages to none; PermissionsManager adds the Quotations row and locks the System Overview module to Admin. `PERMISSIONS_FIXTURE` synced. **The live `settings/permissions` document is unchanged; it needs step 3.3.**
- Coverage map refreshed after Phase 7 step 2 (about 9% of `src` and `api` statements from unit and API tests); roadmap in `TESTING.md` now lists B2 and B6 as the open items.
- Phase 7 step 2.6 (invoice policy): invoice id and type are immutable and only Admin or Manager can change the amount; an invoice with a receipt cannot be deleted and unpaid invoices can be Cancelled instead; the dead `lead.invoiceGenerated` / `invoiceDate` reads are gone (the convert modal derives the Advance invoice from `invoices`); paying a single full-settlement invoice now flips `invoicePaid` (new `invoiceSettlement` helper), on the lead and on the deal it converted into.
- Phase 7 step 2.5 (receipts): `handleGenerateReceipt` refuses an invoice that is not Paid; the receipt amount is read-only (the invoice amount) in Invoices and the lead card, with an optional Notes / Reference field that is forwarded; Receipts disables Export CSV when empty and catches export errors; the delete audit entry falls back to the user's identifier; invoices created from Deals, QuotationBuilder and FabricationWorks carry `partnerId`. The App-level unpaid-invoice guard has no test (not reachable from the UI).
- Phase 7 step 2.4: new `src/utils/dealSettlement.js` (`getFinalInvoiceAmounts`, `calculateDealCommission`). Deal completion bills 25% of the highest-version Accepted quotation (or stored balanceDue, else the deal value), saves that total as `deal.value`, prices the fallback line at the full value, and estimates commission from value (÷850) when the deal has no square footage without adding area. `invoiceTemplate` applies each line's discount and tax before the 25%/75% scaling; the x0.25 print scaling is kept by decision.
- Phase 7 step 2.3: `calculateCODFromInvoices` counts only the latest unpaid Final invoice and returns `finalInvoicePending` with the 25% shortfall for a paid Advance with no Final; Logistics card, details panel and waybill show "Pending 25% Settlement Invoice Creation" instead of "all settled". Two characterisation tests flipped.
- Phase 7 step 2.2: Deals and Fabrication no longer offer a backward move out of Completed; bulk stage change rejects Completed; deal completion records `commissionAccrued` and never accrues partner commission twice. No test for the bulk-change rejection (table selection UI not exercised).
- Phase 7 step 2.1: `getExistingFinalInvoice` in `entityUtils`; deal completion and job QA pass no longer create a second Final invoice (FabricationWorks now receives `invoices`), and the quotation "25% Final Settlement" button shows only for a Deal or after the Advance is paid. The guard is client-side, so a race between two sessions is still possible. Two B5 characterisation tests flipped.
- Phase 7 step 1 (quick wins): sign-out clears notifications; mobile dock uses the `projects` module and Partners get a Profile button instead of Messages; the WhatsApp update and route-insight AI calls go through `generateText` (bearer token attached); registration email is trimmed and lowercased; the notification feed drops the user's own messages; removed the dead `onUnreadCountChange` prop and the false "encrypted" copy in Messages.
