# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Phase 7 step 6.4c (inspection): the fabrication card's QC sign-off milestone can no longer be ticked by hand and a save can never set `qaPassed` (only the QA Inspection Gate can, via `checklistWithGuardedQa`, `src/utils/qaGate.js`); the fabrication card no longer shows the contract value; the QA inspector is always the signed-in user (read-only field); each defect is kept in `defectHistory` (with an id) while `defectDetails` holds the active one and is cleared on a QA pass; `QA_PASSED`, `QA_DEFECT_FLAGGED` and `QA_REWORK_COMPLETED` are audit logged; a job is not marked Completed if its Final invoice fails to save (`handleSaveInvoice` now returns success or failure); the QA invoice picks up the customer phone and company from every alias on the job. Already done earlier: duplicate-Final guard and re-inspection (2.1), AI token (F-5), Operations invoice create permission (3.2). **Not done, in the `PLAN.md` backlog:** notifying the deal's sales owner on Revision (needs stored notifications) and the optional "Email client: QA passed" action.
- Phase 7 step 6.4b, part 2 (fabrication F-3, F-4, F-6; owner decisions 2026-09-21): the size measured on the lead (Length and Height in feet, saved with its pricing) now flows to the deal and to the fabrication job as frame width and height in millimetres and is read-only in the fabrication card, so it is never re-entered downstream (`dimensionsFromLead`, `src/utils/fabricationLink.js`; jobs from leads with no saved size stay editable). The manual job form has no price field: a manual job must be linked to a deal (it inherits the deal's customer and locked size and is billed through the deal) or explicitly marked non-billable internal / extra work, and is refused otherwise before a job number is used. Blueprint uploads go to Firebase Storage and keep only the URL, falling back to inline data under 500KB if Storage refuses and rejecting larger files. **Blueprint upload to Storage has not been tried against the live Storage rules.** Nothing else in Fabrication shows prices.
- Phase 7 step 6.4b (fabrication; F-8, F-9, F-11 and the F-3 sizing fix): the fabrication card no longer sizes a job with only a contract area off a frame 67% of that area (`defaultFrameDimensions` gives a 3:2 frame of the full area); the blueprint draws the real stiffener ribs from the cut-list (`vRibCount` / `hRibCount`, now returned by `calculateCutList`) instead of a fixed X-brace and states the tolerance as ±2.0mm (Structural Steel); the Admin check for deleting a job is case-insensitive and deletions write a `PROJECT_DELETED` audit entry; new projects (lead conversion and manual jobs) carry `customerId` (the customer's email, else NIC) so the customer-portal rule on `projects` can match. Already done earlier: F-1, F-2 (2.x), F-5 (AI proxy sends the token), F-7 (6.2), F-10 (6.6). **Not done here:** F-4 (manual-job Advance invoice, needs an owner decision), F-6 (blueprints to Firebase Storage, needs Storage rules), F-3's dimension capture on leads (leads have no frame size fields). All three are in the `PLAN.md` backlog.
- Phase 7 step 6.4a (deals; D-6, D-9, D-10 and the invoice reprint fix): moving a deal forward now moves its linked fabrication project forward too (Fabricating to Ongoing, Ready To Load to Ready For Inspection, Completed to Completed; matched by job number, never pulls a project back, `src/utils/dealProjectSync.js`); `Hand Over` deals appear in the dashboard action queue; deleting a deal writes a `DEAL_DELETED` audit entry, releases its original lead (`convertedToDeal: false`) and marks the project `Cancelled` with a reason, and never deletes invoices, receipts or logistics. Invoice reprint from the lead card now prints the saved invoice as issued (line items, customer name, company, phone) instead of mixing in the newest quotation and the lead's current details (`src/utils/invoicePrintData.js`); drafts are unchanged. The deals checklist D-1 to D-10 is complete (the rest were done in 2.x, 3.5 and 6.6). **A `Cancelled` project has no Fabrication column, so it leaves the board.** Still to come for 6.4: fabrication (6.4b) and inspection (6.4c).
- Phase 7 step 6.2 (atomic ids): lead ids (`L-0001`), deal ids (`D-0001`), job numbers (`PTF-0001`, from Leads convert and Fabrication), quotation ids (`QT-0001`) and delivery job ids (`L-DL-0001`, from Deals) now come from `generateAtomicId` (a Firestore transaction on `counters/<prefix>`) instead of the last digits of the clock, so two people acting in the same moment can no longer overwrite each other's record or link the wrong ids. If the counter cannot be reached the action stops with an error instead of falling back to a clock id. `firestore.rules` (NOT deployed) adds `L` and `D` to the known counter prefixes; the live rules let any signed-in user write counters, so this works today. **Not converted:** the public referral form (`LD-`) and partner registration (`APP-`) ids, because their users are not signed in and cannot write counters. Old ids (`L-123456`) stay as they are; a new counter could in theory reach one, which needs about 100,000 records.
- Phase 7 step 6.1 (leads, part 2 of 2): the Partners screen no longer lists a converted lead stub (its locked `Completed` stage made the commission look payable and it was counted alongside its Deal), so eligibility follows the Deal; new deals get a collision-checked `jobNo`; lead create, update and convert write audit log entries (`LEAD_CREATED`, `LEAD_UPDATED`, `LEAD_CONVERTED`); the dead Convert button in the Completed column is removed. A Deal now counts as payable only when its invoices are fully paid (or `referralStatus` is set), no longer on stage alone. Invoices are matched through the lead lineage (`getLineageIds` / `invoicesForLineage` in `src/utils/leadLineage.js`: the record's own id, `originalLeadId` and `convertedDealId`), because an invoice can be keyed by either the original lead id or the Deal id. Three new component tests and two unit tests.
- Phase 7 step 6.1 (leads, part 1 of 2): "Convert to Deal" in the lead card only shows for leads in the `Received` stage; saving a quotation sets the lead's `value` to the quote's grand total; saving a lead no longer creates a customer or bumps `orders` (customers are created only when the lead is converted); oversized compressed call recordings (MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, WEBM over 3.2MB) are downsampled like WAV instead of being sent raw and risking a 413. Not in this PR: partner commission gating (Action B), lead audit logging, `jobNo` collisions, the dead Completed-column Convert button. No component test covers the stage gate (the lead card is not yet under test).
- Phase 7 step 5.1 (Google scopes): sign-in keeps the identity scopes only. Drive and Contacts access is requested on demand through `getScopedAccessToken(scope)` in `src/services/firebase.js` (a Google popup for that one scope, hinted with the signed-in email), cached per scope in `sessionStorage` for 55 minutes because Google tokens last an hour and Firebase does not refresh them, and cleared on sign-out. `driveService` (`drive.readonly`) and `contactsService` (`contacts.readonly`) use it, so both no longer fail with 403. The Drive and Contacts screens open a Google consent popup the first time they are used, so they must be started from a click.
- Phase 7 step 6.3 (customers): new `normalizePhone` and `phonesMatch` in `src/utils/validation.js` (spaced, `07` and `+94` spellings of one number are equal, empty never matches). Lead-to-customer matching in `Leads.jsx` (save and convert), the customer stats lookup, and the Google Contacts import in `Customers.jsx` now compare normalised phones, so spaced numbers no longer create duplicate customers. The contact import also no longer skips every phone-only contact because `"" === ""`. One B2 characterisation test flipped.
- Phase 7 step 6.6 (pricing): `calculateCost(tier, sqFt, discountPct = 0, commissionRate = 0)` has no hidden 15% discount and no fixed 53.5 commission, and Profit / SQ is gross profit per sq ft. The Cost Calculator gets Discount % and Partner commission inputs; the lead card uses the partner's rate and 15% only for referral leads. Quotations: a new version becomes the active quote immediately, attached Drive files are saved and restored, and generating the Advance invoice marks the quote Invoiced (treated as Accepted everywhere via `isAcceptedQuote`). Stock-bar orders use first-fit-decreasing packing instead of a 5% linear allowance. Three B1 characterisation tests flipped. **Quotes for direct leads now come out higher than before (no 15% discount) but exclude the old 53.5 commission.** A referral lead whose partner has no commission rate is quoted at LKR 30.00 per sq ft (`getQuotePricingTerms` in `src/utils/quotePricing.js`), the lead's `pricingMetadata.commissionRateDefaulted` is set, and a warning notification asks an Admin or Manager to update the partner's rate.
